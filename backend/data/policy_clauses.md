## Clause 1: Room Rent, Boarding and Associated Medical Expenses Limit

- **Clause ID:** STAR-PREMIER-ROOM-001
- **Insurer:** Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited
- **Product Name:** Star Health Premier Insurance Policy
- **UIN:** SHAHLIP22226V012122
- **Document Version:** POL / SHP / V.6 / 2023
- **Section / Code:** Section B. Coverage - 1. In-Patient Hospitalization (a)
- **Printed PDF Page:** 3 (Associated Medical Expenses definition: PDF page 2)
- **Source PDF URL:** https://web.starhealth.in/sites/default/files/policy-clauses/Health-Premier-Insurance-Policy.pdf
- **Clause Title:** Room Rent, Boarding and Associated Medical Expenses Limit

### Explanation
Hospital room, boarding, and nursing charges are covered up to 1% of the Sum Insured or ₹20,000 per day, whichever is less. (Note on Sum Insured: Under Star Health Premier Insurance Policy, published sum-insured options start at ₹10,00,000 (₹10 lakh); the demonstration scenario uses ₹10 lakh sum insured, corresponding to ₹10,000/day eligible room rent, subject to the policy schedule). If a room category with a rent higher than this entitled limit is occupied, the insured person bears a ratable proportion of the Associated Medical Expenses (such as surgeon, anesthetist, doctor consultation fees, operation theatre charges, and procedure charges that vary by room category, defined on PDF page 2) in proportion to the difference between the entitled room rent and actual room rent. Proportionate deductions are NOT applied where the hospital does not follow differential billing or where differential billing is not adopted based on room category. Furthermore, Associated Medical Expenses explicitly exclude pharmacy and consumables, implants and medical devices, and diagnostics.

### Exact Policy Excerpt
Room, boarding and nursing expenses as provided by the Hospital / Nursing Home up to 1% of the Sum Insured or ₹20,000/- per day, whichever is less.
If the Insured Person occupies a room with a room rent higher than the entitled limit as mentioned above, then the Insured Person shall bear a ratable proportion of the Associated Medical Expenses in the proportion of the difference between the Room Rent of the entitled room category to the Room Rent actually incurred, unless the hospital does not follow differential billing or the differential billing is not adopted based on the room category.
Associated Medical Expenses (defined on PDF page 2) include:
1. Professional fees (including Doctor / Surgeon / Anesthetist / Consultation fees).
2. Operation theatre charges.
3. Procedure charges.
4. Other expenses varying according to room category.
Associated Medical Expenses shall NOT include:
1. Pharmacy and consumables.
2. Implants and medical devices.
3. Cost of diagnostics.

## Clause 2: Specified Disease / Procedure Waiting Period (Code - Excl 02)

- **Clause ID:** STAR-PREMIER-WAIT-002
- **Insurer:** Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited
- **Product Name:** Star Health Premier Insurance Policy
- **UIN:** SHAHLIP22226V012122
- **Document Version:** POL / SHP / V.6 / 2023
- **Section / Code:** Section C. Standard Exclusions - Code - Excl 02
- **Printed PDF Page:** 6
- **Source PDF URL:** https://web.starhealth.in/sites/default/files/policy-clauses/Health-Premier-Insurance-Policy.pdf
- **Clause Title:** Specified Disease / Procedure Waiting Period (Code - Excl 02)

### Explanation
A mandatory 24-month continuous-coverage waiting period applies from the inception date of the first policy before coverage starts for specifically listed illnesses, surgical procedures, and medical treatments (documented on PDF page 6). Specifically included in this list is all types of management for Kidney calculi and Genitourinary tract calculi (this clause is specific to listed conditions and calculi, not generalized to every kidney-related condition). The 24-month waiting period does not apply to claims arising strictly from accidents. In case of sum insured enhancement, this exclusion applies afresh to the enhanced amount. Continuous coverage without break under IRDAI portability norms is credited toward this waiting period. If any listed condition also falls under the pre-existing disease waiting period, the longer of the two waiting periods shall apply.

### Exact Policy Excerpt
Expenses related to the treatment of the listed Conditions, surgeries/treatments shall be excluded until the expiry of 24 consecutive months of continuous coverage after the date of inception of the first policy with us. This exclusion shall not be applicable for claims arising due to an accident.
In case of enhancement of sum insured the exclusion shall apply afresh to the extent of sum insured increase.
If any of the specified disease/procedure falls under the waiting period specified for pre-Existing diseases, then the longer of the two waiting periods shall apply.
The waiting period for listed conditions shall apply even if contracted after the inception of or during the policy period.
If the Insured Person is continuously covered without any break as defined under the applicable norms on portability stipulated by IRDAI, then waiting period for the same would be reduced to the extent of prior coverage.
Specific condition listed under Code-Excl 02 includes: All types of management for Kidney calculi and Genitourinary tract calculi.

## Clause 3: Pre-Existing Diseases Waiting Period (Code - Excl 01)

- **Clause ID:** STAR-PREMIER-PED-001
- **Insurer:** Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited
- **Product Name:** Star Health Premier Insurance Policy
- **UIN:** SHAHLIP22226V012122
- **Document Version:** POL / SHP / V.6 / 2023
- **Section / Code:** Section C. Standard Exclusions - Code - Excl 01
- **Printed PDF Page:** 6
- **Source PDF URL:** https://web.starhealth.in/sites/default/files/policy-clauses/Health-Premier-Insurance-Policy.pdf
- **Clause Title:** Pre-Existing Diseases Waiting Period (Code - Excl 01)

### Explanation
Expenses related to the treatment of a Pre-Existing Disease (PED) and its direct complications are excluded from coverage until 24 consecutive months of continuous coverage have elapsed from the date of inception of the first policy with the insurer (documented on PDF page 6). A pre-existing disease is defined as any condition, ailment, injury or disease diagnosed by a physician within 48 months prior to the effective date of the policy issued by the insurer or for which medical advice or treatment was recommended or received within 48 months prior to the effective date. In case of sum insured enhancement, the exclusion applies afresh to the extent of the sum insured increase. Prior continuous coverage is credited in accordance with IRDAI portability norms. Importantly, this exclusion is not an unconditional exclusion for every medical condition; coverage after 24 months is subject to the condition having been declared in the proposal form at the time of application and accepted by the insurer.

### Exact Policy Excerpt
Expenses related to the treatment of a pre-existing Disease (PED) and its direct complications shall be excluded until the expiry of 24 consecutive months of continuous coverage after the date of inception of the first policy with insurer.
In case of enhancement of sum insured the exclusion shall apply afresh to the extent of sum insured increase.
If the Insured Person is continuously covered without any break as defined under the portability norms of the extant IRDAI (Health Insurance) Regulations, then for the said Insured Person, waiting period for the same would be reduced to the extent of prior coverage.
Coverage under the policy after the expiry of 24 months for any pre-existing disease is subject to the same being declared at the time of application and accepted by Insurer.

## Clause 4: Coverage for Modern Treatment (Robotic Surgeries)

- **Clause ID:** STAR-PREMIER-MODERN-014
- **Insurer:** Star Health and Allied Insurance Company Limited
- **Product Name:** Star Health Premier Insurance Policy
- **UIN:** SHAHLIP22226V012122
- **Document Version:** POL / SHP / V.6 / 2023
- **Section / Code:** Section II, Coverage 14 – Coverage for Modern Treatment
- **Printed PDF Page:** 4
- **Source PDF URL:** https://web.starhealth.in/sites/default/files/policy-clauses/Health-Premier-Insurance-Policy.pdf
- **Clause Title:** Coverage for Modern Treatment (Robotic Surgeries)

### Explanation
Expenses incurred for Modern Treatments and advanced procedures, specifically including Robotic Surgeries, are covered under Section II, Coverage 14 up to 50% of the Sum Insured per policy period, including pre-hospitalisation and post-hospitalisation expenses. For the demonstration sum insured of ₹10,00,000 (₹10 lakh), the maximum payable limit under this special coverage is ₹5,00,000 (50% of Sum Insured), subject to applicable policy terms, pre-authorization, and waiting periods. This provision specifically covers advanced robotic surgical procedures and is distinct from general room rent limits and bariatric surgery provisions.

### Exact Policy Excerpt
Coverage for Modern Treatment: The expenses incurred for the following Modern Treatments / Advanced treatments are covered up to 50% of the Sum Insured per policy period, inclusive of pre-hospitalisation and post-hospitalisation expenses:
1. Uterine Artery Embolization and HIFU (High intensity focused ultrasound)
2. Balloon Sinuplasty
3. Deep Brain stimulation
4. Oral chemotherapy
5. Immunotherapy - Monoclonal antibody to be given as injection
6. Intra vitreal injections
7. Robotic surgeries
8. Stereotactic radio surgeries
9. Bronchial Thermoplasty
10. Vaporisation of the prostrate (Green laser treatment or holmium laser treatment)
11. IONM - (Intra Operative Neuro Monitoring)
12. Stem cell therapy: Hematopoietic stem cells for bone marrow transplant for haematological conditions to be covered.
Note: For a ₹10,00,000 Sum Insured schedule, the maximum modern treatment coverage limit is 50% (₹5,00,000). Coverage for robotic surgeries is governed under this clause and is separate from bariatric surgery exclusions.
